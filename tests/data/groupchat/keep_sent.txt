# A retention policy that expires sent plaintext after one epoch, whether or
# not it was acked: the disappearing-messages case. The message becomes a
# tombstone, and a member who had not read it yet only learns it missed it.

option keep-sent 1
new-group alice bob
send alice hello
tick
peek alice 0
----
tombstone

read bob alice
----
alice@0 tombstone
alice@1 not found

scan bob alice           # a tombstone counts as read: nothing to recheck
----
alice@1 not found
